About

Qalm Learning supports students and the people who lead their education. In Australia, we offer small-group maths and English tutoring for K-12 students. Internationally, we design and run workshops for school and university leaders, including in Indonesia.

Our work draws on doctoral research in education at the University of Melbourne, teaching from high school to postgraduate level, and more than 45 research and consulting projects across Australia, Indonesia, New Zealand and the United States.
